Service

With my work, whether it be toward a future career or volunteering in my community, I hope to let everyone that I work with, or toward, that they are safe to be who they want to be. Throughout my academic career, I volunteered as an officer of diversity orgs, specifically focusing on inclusion and accessibility.

During my college career, I volunteered as various officer positions under my campus's Neurodivergent Leadership on Campus. We specifically focused on bringing awareness of various neurodivergencies and mental disabilities, contacted leaders in our local community for close-to-home volunteer opportunities, and worked with guest speakers near and far to have lectures on becoming and being a leader.
