Pokémon Facts Showcase

Chandelure • Gardevoir • Meloetta • Lucario

Chandelure

Type: Ghost / Fire

  • Known as the Luring Pokémon.
  • Its flames consume spirits instead of bodies.
  • Evolves from Lampent using a Dusk Stone.

Gardevoir

Type: Psychic / Fairy

  • Known as the Embrace Pokémon.
  • Can sense its Trainer’s emotions.
  • Will risk its life to protect those it loves.

Meloetta

Type: Normal / Psychic

  • Known as the Melody Pokémon.
  • Its songs can change emotions.
  • Has Aria Forme and Pirouette Forme.

Lucario

Type: Fighting / Steel

  • Known as the Aura Pokémon.
  • Can read aura to understand feelings.
  • Can Mega Evolve into Mega Lucario.
